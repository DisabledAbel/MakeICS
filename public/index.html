TVMaze + optional IMDb enrichment

Find upcoming TV episodes and export them to calendar.

Search a show, see every known upcoming episode, and copy an ICS calendar URL that refreshes daily in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook, and other calendar apps.

The all-time feed uses TVMaze for show and episode schedules and asks calendar apps to refresh every day. It uses the free public IMDb endpoint by default, or FIRECRAWL_API_KEY for Firecrawl-powered IMDb page scraping when configured.
