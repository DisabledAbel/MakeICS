TVMaze + optional IMDb enrichment

Find upcoming TV episodes and export them to calendar.

Search a show, see its next air dates, and copy an ICS calendar URL for Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook, and other calendar apps.

The app uses TVMaze for show and episode schedules. It uses the free public IMDb endpoint by default, or FIRECRAWL_API_KEY for Firecrawl-powered IMDb page scraping when configured.
